# Investor Research Engine

**Status:** In development

The ambition is to build a research workflow that helps investors and advisers evaluate funds and markets using historical evidence, transparent methodology and careful treatment of data limitations.

Research areas include historical mutual fund analysis, survivorship-bias awareness (including closed and merged funds where data exists), point-in-time information, index valuation metrics, data-quality checks and evidence-based fund evaluation.

## Principles
1. Preserve source-data dates and provenance.
2. Avoid look-ahead bias.
3. Include dead, merged or closed funds when required and reliable data exists.
4. Separate exploratory findings from validated conclusions.
5. Show assumptions, limitations and uncertainty.
6. Require human review before investor-facing claims.

This is a foundation for a longer-term AI-powered investor intelligence platform, not a guarantee of investment outcomes.
