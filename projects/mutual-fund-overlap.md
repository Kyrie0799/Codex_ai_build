# Mutual Fund Overlap Tool

**Status:** Product development

Investors may own funds that appear different by name or category but hold many of the same securities. This tool is designed to compare mutual fund portfolios and make shared holdings and potential concentration easier to inspect.

Useful analysis needs clear portfolio dates, consistent security identifiers, treatment of cash and derivatives, and transparent handling of missing holdings. Holdings can be delayed or stale.

Overlap is one dimension of diversification, not a complete measure of risk. Similar holdings do not automatically imply identical risk, and low overlap does not guarantee diversification.
