# Advisoira Mobile — Goal Progress and Financial Behaviour

**Status:** Product work; confirm release status before calling it publicly launched.

The mobile application is designed to help clients track investment progress against financial goals, understand whether they remain on track and log monthly financial behaviour. The goal is to turn financial planning into an ongoing feedback loop rather than a static document.
