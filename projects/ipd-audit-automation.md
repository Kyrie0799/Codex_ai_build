# IPD Audit Automation

**Status:** Internal tool

IPD Audit Automation is an internal Advisoira tool intended to automate aspects of investment portfolio data auditing and reduce repetitive manual work.

Intended value:
- Make recurring audit steps more systematic.
- Reduce avoidable manual effort.
- Support consistency in data review.
- Give the adviser more time for investigation and client-facing work.

Public demos should use synthetic sample records. Do not claim measured accuracy or efficiency improvements without evidence.
