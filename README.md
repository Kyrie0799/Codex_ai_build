# Building with Codex: Advisoira's Financial Technology Portfolio

**Partha Deshpande · Founder, Advisoira · Tallinn, Estonia**

I am a finance professional and founder building financial technology products with Codex. This repository documents the problems I am solving, products I am building, high-level architecture and lessons from AI-assisted development.

> Domain experts should be able to turn deep industry knowledge into useful software. Codex can help close the gap between identifying a problem and shipping a product.

## Selected work

- ETF Pulse — premiums and discounts across 320+ NSE-listed ETFs: https://www.advisoira.com/etf-dashboard.html
- Advisoira Mobile — investment-goal progress and monthly behaviour logging.
- Client Snapshot — structured client information and financial-analysis inputs.
- Advisoira Website — financial tools, risk assessment and newsletter: https://www.advisoira.com
- Backend and integrations — Supabase, GitHub, Resend and APIs.
- Mutual Fund Overlap — holdings overlap and concentration analysis.
- IPD Audit Automation — internal investment-data audit workflow automation.
- Investor Research Engine — historical fund research, valuation and data-quality analysis.

## How I use Codex

Codex has been used across most of these projects to translate requirements into implementation, iterate on workflows and work through technical problems. Financial calculations, data quality, security and user-impacting decisions require explicit validation and human responsibility.

## Next ambition

An AI-powered investor intelligence platform combining reliable financial data, portfolio understanding, historical research and explainable analysis. This is under development, not a claim of a complete autonomous investment-advice product.
