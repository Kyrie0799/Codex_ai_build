# The Codex Development Journey

My background is in finance and financial planning. Working with investors exposed recurring problems that could be improved with software: fragmented client information, hidden portfolio overlap, difficult-to-access ETF analytics and repetitive operational work.

I wanted to solve these problems without coming from a conventional software-engineering background. Codex became part of how I approach product development. Across most Advisoira projects, I have used it to translate requirements into implementation, iterate on workflows and work through technical problems.

The lesson is not that AI makes engineering effortless. It changes the leverage available to a domain expert willing to learn, inspect, test and take responsibility for the result.

## What I will document
1. The problem and constraints.
2. Architecture and trade-offs.
3. Where Codex helped.
4. What required human judgement.
5. Bugs and debugging lessons.
6. Testing and validation.
7. What shipped and what remains unfinished.

I will be specific about what Codex did, protect client data and proprietary code, validate financial calculations, and share failures as well as successes.
